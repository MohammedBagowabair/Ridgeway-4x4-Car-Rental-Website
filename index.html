Rental requirements

What you need to bring

  • Minimum age 23 (25 for Defender, Wrangler and Land Cruiser)
  • Full driving licence held for at least 2 years
  • MyKad, or passport for international travellers
  • International Driving Permit if your licence is not in English or Malay
  • Refundable security deposit, confirmed on WhatsApp for your vehicle
  • Off-road use on approved trails only; ask us about your route

Policies

Clear, simple terms

Mileage

Unlimited mileage within Peninsular Malaysia for rentals of 3 days or more.

Off-road

Light to moderate trails welcome. No river crossings beyond the vehicle's wading depth, and no competitive events.

Fuel

Full to full. Diesel pickups come with a fuel guide card.

Insurance

Comprehensive cover included. Undercarriage and tyre damage from off-road use is the hirer's responsibility.

Cleaning

Mud happens. A basic wash is included; heavy interior cleaning may be charged.

Changes

Free changes up to 48 hours before pick-up. Flight delayed? We wait for you.

FAQ

Good questions

Can I collect the car at KLIA or KLIA2?

Yes, it is our speciality. Choose the terminal, send your flight number, and we will meet you at arrivals, any time of day.

Can I take the car off-road?

Yes, on approved trails and plantation roads. Tell us your route on WhatsApp and we will confirm which vehicle suits it.

Do I need 4WD experience?

No. Every handover includes a quick briefing on 4WD modes, low range and diff locks, and we are on WhatsApp if you need help.

Can I add camping gear or a roof rack?

Yes. Roof racks, cooler boxes, camping chairs and recovery boards are available on request.

Can I drive to the East Coast or Taman Negara?

Yes. Unlimited mileage within Peninsular Malaysia for rentals of 3 days or more.

Which car for Cameron or Genting?

For families, the Fortuner or Pajero Sport. For couples, the Forester or Jimny. For the most comfort, the Land Cruiser.

Where to next?
